How ShipSpree works

From the first shipment details to the final paperwork, ShipSpree keeps the process clear and coordinated.

One accountable freight partner throughout the shipment.

01 — Tell Us What’s Moving
Share the origin, destination, shipment weight, freight type and required pickup date.

02 — We Price and Source Capacity
We review the shipment requirements, prepare the quote and coordinate suitable carrier capacity for the load.

03 — We Coordinate the Shipment
ShipSpree manages the important communication around pickup, transit and delivery so the shipment remains organized.

04 — We Close Out the Load
Delivery documentation and approved charges are brought together so the shipment can be closed out clearly.
